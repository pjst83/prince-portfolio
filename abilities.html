Experience

What activity or hobby do you feel has prepared you the most for a possible career? How?

I believe that my experience in music production (creating music from a young age under a lot of different monikers through the years) and composition (my childhood of playing piano) has prepared me the most for a possible career in music technology or audio engineering. It has taught me how to use various software tools, understand sound design principles, and collaborate effectively with others on creative projects. It has also led me to understand the importance of space and echos and how they effect mood in music. THis can be found in the perfect albums "Bon Iver - For Emma, Forever Ago" and "The Beatles - Abbey Road" which both use reverb and space in unique ways to create distinct atmospheres in their music.
